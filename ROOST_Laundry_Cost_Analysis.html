Action Plan

Six Prioritized Recommendations

$105K – $170K Modeled
01
Highest Impact · Detroit

Renegotiate Luster contract — convert to per-pound and cap specialty pricing

Detroit's $190,947 annual spend is the largest in the portfolio. Luster bills sheets/towels per piece (~$0.70/pc) and charges $15 per duvet/insert — 5–11× the rest of the portfolio. Convert the wash component to per-pound at $0.70/lb (TenFold parity) and cap duvets at $5/pc. Same-day ($200) and rush ($100) trip fees should be eliminated or capped at 2/month. Owner: Scott Sadoff / Patrick Jobst.

$40,000 – $60,000 / yr
02
Quick Win · Philadelphia

Pressure-test Single Source on multi-property rate & delivery fee

SS Plus services both Midtown and East Market on identical $0.80/lb pricing with a $300–$375 flat delivery fee per service day — the highest fixed cost in the portfolio. Negotiate a $0.65/lb multi-property rate (Pinnacle parity) and a $200 flat delivery, or formally RFP the Phila pair. Single Source's competitive risk is the leverage.

$20,000 – $30,000 / yr
03
Strategic · Mid-Atlantic

Issue a Mid-Atlantic regional RFP for one consolidated vendor

Four ROOSTs (Midtown, East Market, Baltimore, White House) in a 200-mile corridor combine for ~$269K of annual spend. A single vendor could profitably underbid the current $0.65–$0.80 spread. Target rate: $0.60/lb plus $100 flat delivery. Pinnacle and Mayflower are natural incumbents to invite; SS Plus should defend.

$30,000 – $45,000 / yr
04
Operational · Tampa

Investigate Tampa monthly cost volatility ($5.8K → $21.9K)

Crown Linen's $0.468/lb headline rate is the portfolio's best, yet Tampa's monthly cost ranges from $5,849 to $21,857 — a 3.7× swing. Possible drivers: pool/F&B linen, banquet events, or specialty (duvet/blanket) volume spikes. Audit two outlier months (May 2025 at $11,728 vs Apr 2025 at $20,709) to isolate cost drivers and either price-cap specialty or shift to par-stock owned linen.

$10,000 – $25,000 / yr
05
Hygiene · All Vendors

Standardize contract terms — pricing transparency & benchmarking clauses

Current contracts span Net 10 (TenFold) to Net 30 (most), with inconsistent fuel/energy surcharges, unclear minimum-charge logic (Mayflower), and one 5%/month late-fee clause (Pinnacle). Standardize on Net 30 with capped fuel surcharge (≤2%), eliminate minimums, and require an annual rate-disclosure clause. Adds optionality without changing vendors.

$5,000 – $10,000 / yr
06
Visibility · GM Scorecard

Make $/Occupied Room a monthly KPI for each GM

Portfolio variance is wide ($3.99 Baltimore → $8.78 Midtown). Some spread is structural (small-property delivery economics), but a monthly $/room report — alongside the property's vendor invoice spend by category — gives every GM real-time accountability and surfaces drift early. Set a $5.00/room "watch line" and $4.50/room target tied to next-cycle property planning. Aligns with Method Co.'s Relentless Pursuit of Excellence.
